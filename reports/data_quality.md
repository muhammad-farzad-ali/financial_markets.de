## Notes

- The collector is throttled to one request per second and retries HTTP 429/5xx with exponential backoff. Responses are cached for six hours; `--refresh` forces a new download.
- `missing_business_days` compares calendar weekdays with observed bars. Exchange holidays, half-days and source gaps explain most differences; it is not an error count. The issue threshold is 5% of expected business days (minimum 10 days).
- OHLC consistency is only counted for equities and indices. FX and rate series use different high/low conventions; their closes are unaffected.
- The analysis uses the DAX trading calendar as the reference. FX/rate weekend dates remain in the raw TSV but are excluded from correlations, breadth and group indices.
- The DAX chart endpoint can lag the latest session by one day. The collector appends the current quote when its date is newer than the last daily bar.
- CNBC quotes are delayed and the endpoints are unofficial; the pipeline is designed to fail softly and keep the last successful data.
- Wikipedia is used only for the DAX composition and sector labels; the NACE mapping is a curated static file in `config/nace_map.tsv`.
